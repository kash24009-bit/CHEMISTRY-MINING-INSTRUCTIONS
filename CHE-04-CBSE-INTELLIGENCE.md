## 3 · How answer books are marked NOW (board-wide; VERIFIED in the 2026 Physics marking scheme's general instructions; Batch 0 confirms them in this subject's 2026 scheme)

1. **On-Screen Marking (OSM)** for Class XII from the 2026 exam. Answer books are scanned and marked on screen; the system totals the marks.
2. **Both options now count.** If a candidate attempts both alternatives of an internal-choice question, both are marked and the system keeps the higher score.
3. **An error is penalised only once:** no marks are deducted for the cumulative effect of an error.
4. **Competency answers:** in Class XII, a correct competency enumerated by the candidate earns due marks even if the reply is not from the marking scheme.
5. **Value points are guidelines, not the complete answer.** A student's own correct expression earns the marks.
6. **The full scale must be used:** full marks when the answer deserves them.
7. **Examiner workload:** about 20 answer books a day in main subjects, so answers must be easy to mark: numbered, labelled, results boxed, units present.
8. **Reading time:** 15 minutes; writing starts at 10:30.

## 6 · Identity clues inside the files

- **Paper codes:** Chemistry is series 56 (56/1/1, 56/2/3 …); the kit already knows the 55, 56, 65 and 30 families. Visually-impaired papers carry letter codes; compartment papers sit in "COMPTT" folders. The first paper's page 1 confirms the series.
- **2026 marking-scheme footers carry an internal code `YY-SS-SSN`.** `26-03-43N` was seen on a real 2026 Chemistry scheme: 2026, series 3, subject 43. It proves the year even when the file name doesn't.
- **One scheme can cover several sets,** for example "Q.P.CODE 56_2_1,2,3", with underscores instead of slashes.
- **File names lie constantly.** In the first Chemistry project, 80 of 81 names had no year; "…Main_paper.pdf" was a 2023 marking scheme; "…English.pdf" was a 2026 marking scheme; "56_4_2.pdf" was the scheme for 56/4/1-2-3. The setup and the kit identify files by content, never by name.
