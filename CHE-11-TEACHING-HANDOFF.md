# CHE-11 · TEACHING HANDOFF — Chemistry (043)
*What her Chemistry teacher needs from this account, from the Professors' own lists at the staff meeting of 2026-11-20. This account has no memory of her classes; this file is that memory.*

## The pack: the unit of delivery
- **One file per chapter:** `outputs/packs/CHE-PACK-CH<nn>.md`, stamped with its coverage ("based on 2026–2025"), rebuilt each time a year closes.
- **Each question carries:** year · set (paper code) · question number · marks · the question as printed · the marking scheme's own lines, word for word, each with its mark · its pattern (code and plain name).
- **The last section is Test-bank ready:** the same questions with their KEY lines, because her Sunday tests use only banked questions.
- **Few files:** her study projects break when a long list of files is added, so one chapter is one file and nothing else is needed to use it.
- **How it reaches her class:** the file lives in this repository on GitHub, which both her accounts can reach; she adds that one file to the class project, or downloads and attaches it.
- **When:** about 14 days before teaching reaches the chapter. Her teachers set the order; she names the chapter when she asks.
- **Never** invent a question to fill a gap; a thin pack says how thin, with the numbers.

## What Chemistry needs (Professor Chemistry)
- **Keep these families apart:** conversions; named reactions; give-reasons and account-for; distinguishing tests; mechanisms; arrange-in-order; IUPAC names and structures; numericals in Solutions, Electrochemistry and Kinetics.
- **Write out structures and reactions in full:** every structure as its name plus a condensed formula; every H on the reacting centre; every arrow with its reagent and condition. What can't be read is UNREADABLE, never guessed.
- **Every set,** not one per region.
- **Keep scheme lines whole.** Organic is 33 of the 70 marks, and its marks hide in exact scheme lines: reagent, condition, reason.
- **First wanted:** the Ch 6 Haloalkanes and Haloarenes pack, then the chapters in her teaching order.

## The rule this account enforces on the teachers
- Professors propose; only a printed page proves. A pattern a teacher suggests stays DRAFT until a real question verifies it.
