# PYQ Pattern Mining · Chemistry (043)

Every CBSE Class 12 Chemistry board paper and marking scheme she supplies, mined into a verified question ledger with patterns, to predict the 2027 paper, write blueprint-exact mocks and give her Chemistry classes one pack per chapter. Ported on 2026-11-20 from her Physics (042) project, with every rule it learned; it runs on her mining account so her lessons keep their own.

## How it starts
1. She adds, once, to this repository: the official 2026-27 syllabus PDF and the 2026-27 sample paper with its marking scheme; and the year's papers and schemes in their own repositories, as for Physics.
2. In a session on this repository she types `Batch 0.`; then `Go.` each session after that.
